FM-1 add-ons

Knobs and a faceplate with your own text for the M-VAVE FM-1. Change them here, in your browser, and download the STLs to print.

Printing

Print each part the way it comes, the side that sits on the FM-1 down, without supports (PLA or PETG). The defaults fit the FM-1: they're tested on one. If your printer makes a knob loose or tight, print with Fit a little tighter (less) if a knob is loose, or looser if it won't go on.

For a two-color faceplate, load Plate and Text together as one object with two parts, and give each its filament. Without a multi-material printer, raised text works with a filament change at the layer the text starts. Engraved text's Text part fills the engraving: an inlay, flush with the plate.

Sizes are in mm. Settings are kept in this browser. The OpenSCAD file is in the repo, to use in OpenSCAD itself.
